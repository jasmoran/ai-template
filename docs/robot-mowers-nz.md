# Robot lawnmowers available in NZ (research, Nov 2026)

Compiled 2026-11-17 from NZ retailer listings (Mowbot, Robomate, PriceSpy, Harvey Norman NZ, JB Hi-Fi NZ, PB Tech, Stihl Shop NZ, Bunnings NZ, City Honda) and Husqvarna/Segway NZ pages. Prices are NZD incl. GST and change often; Black Friday sales were on at the time.
Not verified: Consumer NZ scores are paywalled; the only scores found came from Segway's own blog. Edge and area figures marked "?" are from general product knowledge and were not confirmed for that exact model.

## Table

Edge: **Yes** = dedicated edge blade/trimmer; **Partial** = can cut close only where it can drive over a flush edge, or claims a ~5 cm gap; **No** = expect to trim a strip by hand.
Wire: **No** = RTK/GPS, camera or LiDAR virtual boundary; **EPOS add-on** = Husqvarna ships wired, wire-free needs the EPOS module (~$769, free with some models until 30 Nov 2026).

## Extra columns suggested to the user

Slope rating and drive (2WD/AWD), navigation type and what it needs (RTK base station, network RTK/4G subscription, clear sky view under trees), narrow-passage and multi-zone handling, cut height range, obstacle/hedgehog/pet safety, rain and IP rating, noise, anti-theft (GPS/4G, PIN), ongoing costs (subscriptions, blades), NZ warranty and local service.
